# 🏭 Computour

> **Making industrial and factory visits easier, safer, and more accessible through immersive virtual tours.**

**Computour** is a fully localized virtual tour solution designed for factories, industrial facilities, companies, and high-risk environments. It enables organizations to create immersive digital versions of their environments, allowing people to explore them virtually — without the cost, complexity, and safety concerns of physical visits.

## 🚀 What is Computour?

Visiting industrial facilities and high-risk environments can be difficult, expensive, and sometimes unsafe.

Computour addresses this challenge by creating **immersive virtual tours of real-world environments**, allowing organizations to present their facilities digitally and make them accessible from anywhere, at any time.

The solution can be delivered through:

* 🥽 **VR headsets** for immersive experiences
* 🌐 **Websites** for easy access from anywhere

This makes it possible to experience an industrial environment without physically being there.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Physical visits to factories and industrial environments often come with significant challenges:

* 🚧 **Safety concerns** in high-risk environments
* 💰 **Travel and transportation costs** for visitors
* 👷 **The need for trained personnel** to guide and explain the environment
* 🏭 **Production interruptions** during certain visits
* 🛡️ **Additional safety arrangements** for visitors
* 🌍 **Limited accessibility** for people who are in other cities or countries
* ⏰ **Limited availability** of physical visits

In many cases, even organizing a single visit requires significant preparation, coordination, and cost.

### Computour changes the model.

Instead of bringing every visitor to the facility, **bring the facility to the visitor.**

---

## 💡 Why Computour?

Computour is not simply a 360° view of an environment.

It is designed as a **customizable virtual experience** that can be adapted to different organizations, audiences, and use cases.

### Key capabilities

* 🎯 **Fully localized for each organization**
* 🧩 **Deep customization of the virtual environment**
* 🗺️ **Interactive maps and navigation**
* 📋 **Custom interactive menus**
* 🎙️ **Voice-over and narration**
* 🎬 **Scenario-based experiences**
* 👨‍🏫 **Educational and training scenarios**
* 👥 **Different experiences for different audiences**
* 🥽 **Deployment on VR headsets**
* 🌐 **Deployment on websites**

---

## ⭐ What Makes Computour Different?

### 🇮🇷 Fully Localized

Computour is designed to be adapted to the specific needs, environment, identity, and requirements of each organization.

The experience can be customized instead of forcing every organization into a predefined structure.

### 🔐 Local Data

For organizations that are highly sensitive about their information, Computour can keep the required information **locally within the project environment**.

This makes it suitable for organizations where data privacy and control are important considerations.

### 🧩 Full Customization

Different organizations have different environments, audiences, and goals.

Computour allows different parts of the experience to be customized based on the organization's needs — from navigation and menus to scenarios and presentation.

### 🎬 Scenario-Based Experiences

A virtual tour doesn't have to be the same for everyone.

Different scenarios can be designed for different audiences and objectives, including:

* Educational tours
* Training experiences
* Visitor-specific scenarios
* Guided presentations
* Specialized industrial walkthroughs

## ❓ FAQ

### Is Computour open source?

No. Computour is currently a private project. This repository is intended to introduce the project, its capabilities, and its vision.

### Does Computour require a VR headset?

Not necessarily.

Virtual tours can be delivered through **VR headset Meta Quest or websites**, depending on the requirements of the project.

### Can the experience be customized for different organizations?

Yes. Customization is one of the core concepts of Computour. Different environments, navigation structures, scenarios, audiences, and presentation methods can be adapted to the needs of each organization.

### Can sensitive company information remain local?

Computour is designed with local data handling in mind for organizations that require greater control over their information.

### Who can benefit from Computour?

Factories, industrial companies, organizations with high-risk environments, educational institutions, and any organization that wants to provide remote or virtual access to a physical environment can potentially benefit from Computour.

## 🚀 What is Computour?

Visiting industrial facilities and high-risk environments can be difficult, expensive, and sometimes unsafe.

Computour addresses this challenge by creating **immersive virtual tours of real-world environments**, allowing organizations to present their facilities digitally and make them accessible from anywhere, at any time.

The solution can be delivered through:

* 🥽 **VR headsets** for immersive experiences
* 🌐 **Websites** for easy access from anywhere

This makes it possible to experience an industrial environment without physically being there.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Physical visits to factories and industrial environments often come with significant challenges:

* 🚧 **Safety concerns** in high-risk environments
* 💰 **Travel and transportation costs** for visitors
* 👷 **The need for trained personnel** to guide and explain the environment
* 🏭 **Production interruptions** during certain visits
* 🛡️ **Additional safety arrangements** for visitors
* 🌍 **Limited accessibility** for people who are in other cities or countries
* ⏰ **Limited availability** of physical visits

In many cases, even organizing a single visit requires significant preparation, coordination, and cost.

### Computour changes the model.

Instead of bringing every visitor to the facility, **bring the facility to the visitor.**

---

## 💡 Why Computour?

Computour is not simply a 360° view of an environment.

It is designed as a **customizable virtual experience** that can be adapted to different organizations, audiences, and use cases.

## ⭐ What Makes Computour Different?

### 🇮🇷 Fully Localized

Computour is designed to be adapted to the specific needs, environment, identity, and requirements of each organization.

The experience can be customized instead of forcing every organization into a predefined structure.

### 🔐 Local Data

For organizations that are highly sensitive about their information, Computour can keep the required information **locally within the project environment**.

This makes it suitable for organizations where data privacy and control are important considerations.

### 🧩 Full Customization

Different organizations have different environments, audiences, and goals.

Computour allows different parts of the experience to be customized based on the organization's needs — from navigation and menus to scenarios and presentation.

### 🎬 Scenario-Based Experiences

A virtual tour doesn't have to be the same for everyone.

Different scenarios can be designed for different audiences and objectives, including:

* Educational tours
* Training experiences
* Visitor-specific scenarios
* Guided presentations
* Specialized industrial walkthroughs
