# Computour

### **Solving the difficulty of visiting factories, industries, and companies using virtual tours**

**Computour** is a fully localized solution for creating virtual tours of factories, industrial environments, companies, and high-risk environments; a solution that helps organizations make their environment available to audiences at any time and from any location, without requiring physical presence.

## What is Computour?

Visiting factories, industrial environments, and especially high-risk environments is never easy or low-cost.

**Computour** enables people to interactively experience and explore an industrial or organizational environment without physical presence, by creating virtual tours of real environments.

These virtual tours can be presented in two ways:

* **On virtual reality headsets** to create an immersive experience
* **On a website** for easy access from any location and without the need for specialized equipment

This way, instead of always taking people to the environment, we can **bring the environment to the people.**

---

## What problem does it solve?

Physical visits to factories and industrial environments are usually accompanied by various challenges:

*  Presence of hazards and safety restrictions
*  Travel and visitor presence costs
*  Need for trained personnel to explain the environment on every visit
*  Possibility of needing to stop or disrupt the production line
*  Need to provide safety conditions for visitors
*  Impossibility of easy visits for people located in another city or country
*  Limited time and conditions for conducting in-person visits

In many cases, even holding a simple visit requires significant coordination, cost, and preparation.

### Computour changes this model.

Instead of bringing people to the factory or industrial environment for every visit, we can **provide them with a virtual version of that environment.**

---

## Why Computour?

Computour is not merely a 360-degree image of an environment.

The goal is to create an **interactive and customizable virtual experience** that can be modified based on the type of organization, audience, and purpose of the visit.

### Full localization

Computour is designed with the aim of addressing the real needs of various organizations and environments.

The structure of the virtual tour can change according to the environment, identity, needs, and goals of each organization.

### Full customization

Every organization has a different environment, audience, and purpose.

For this reason, Computour is designed in such a way that different parts of the experience—from how the environment and menus are displayed to the navigation path and scenarios—are customizable.

### Local data storage

For companies and organizations that are sensitive about their environment and organizational information, it is possible to store the required information **completely locally within the project**.

This feature provides greater control over information and reduces dependence on external services.

### Different scenarios

A virtual environment does not necessarily have to provide the same experience for everyone.

Different scenarios can be defined for different audiences and purposes; for example:

* Educational visits
* Personnel training
* Introducing the factory to guests
* Presentation to customers or business partners
* Specialized visits
* Introducing different parts of an organization

### Output on virtual reality headsets

Capability of implementation on Meta Quest virtual reality headsets
